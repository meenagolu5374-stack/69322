Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before taking up handwriting copy assignments

Are there any registration or deposit fees? +
No! Vishav Book does not charge any registration fees, security deposits, or file charges. All notebooks and work materials are delivered free to your location.
How do I receive my payment? +
Once your completed notebooks are delivered and verified by our quality inspection team, payments are processed immediately via direct UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) or NEFT Bank Transfer.
What kind of handwriting is required? +
Standard, legible handwriting is all you need. You do not need professional calligraphy — just clean, neat, readable sentences with regular margins.
Can college students manage this along with studies? +
Yes, our projects are specifically structured for students. Writing 4 to 8 pages per day in your free evening hours easily completes projects well before the deadline.
